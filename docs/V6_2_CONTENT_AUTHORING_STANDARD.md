# v6.2 Content Authoring Standard

## Reference model
The curriculum uses observable CEFR-style can-do outcomes rather than assigning a level from vocabulary count alone. German pathways use Goethe A1–C2 competence orientation and four-skill logic. Elena’s pathway uses positive, visual and familiar-task principles consistent with young-learner assessment. Arya’s PRE pathway is deliberately not treated as a formal CEFR examination route.

## Mandatory lesson contract
Every authored lesson must include:
1. unique ID, member, target language and level;
2. Persian and target-language titles;
3. one observable objective and one can-do statement;
4. age-appropriate duration;
5. reviewed vocabulary/chunks and model phrases;
6. at least three interactive tasks;
7. at least one productive or participatory output;
8. a real-life or project mission;
9. an explicit rubric or participation observation;
10. safeguarding metadata for children.

## Language quality controls
- No fabricated citations, statistics or journal status.
- No claim that P90 is accident probability.
- Research facts for Saeed must match the locked thesis and manuscript data.
- German nouns carry articles in beginner vocabulary.
- English and German are authored independently; literal machine translation is not accepted.
- C-level content must require synthesis, qualification, register and source integration.
- Original texts only; textbook, commercial-exam, song and article content must not be copied.

## Age policy
### Adults
Functional communication, explicit reflection, productive writing/speaking and authentic missions.

### Elena, age 11
Visual tasks, phonics, reading, stories, science, school projects and digital safety. Private-by-default evidence; no public sharing of child data.

### Arya, age 3
Parent-guided 5–8 minute encounters. Attention, pointing, movement, enjoyment and voluntary imitation. No grammar teaching, written homework, formal score or forced repetition.

## Foundation Pack target
The first controlled tranche targets:
- Saeed: research English, IELTS/PhD communication and foundational German;
- Arzoo: English and German A1 for shopping, kitchen, school and IT;
- Elena: English and German A0/A1 visual, phonics, school, story, science and digital safety;
- Arya: English and German PRE parent-guided visual and movement sessions.

This tranche is an authored foundation and is not the complete A0–C2 corpus.
